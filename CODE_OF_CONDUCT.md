# Code of Conduct

We want Hazen's community to be welcoming and respectful.

**Do:** be kind, assume good intent, give specific and constructive feedback, respect other people's time and privacy.
**Don't:** harass, insult, discriminate, threaten, post private information, or spam.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules. Report problems to aswanthr82@gmail.com; reports are handled privately.
This applies to the repository, issues, discussions and any space where someone represents the project.
