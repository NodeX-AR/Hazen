## Full description
Hazen is a pro camera, photo album and editor in one app.

CAMERA
- Photo, Video, Portrait, Ultra (multi-frame 4K) and Night modes
- Lens-ring zoom, manual controls, histogram, zebra, focus peaking, level and grid
- Looks and custom .cube LUTs, Auto Enhance that keeps your look
- On-device AI: face lock, smile and gesture shutter, blink guard, portrait blur

EDITOR
- Crop, straighten, adjust, looks, draw, text and stickers
- Video trim, speed, colour, fades and a Fairlight-style audio mixer (EQ, compressor, pan, meter)
- AI tools: background blur, cut out subject, smart frame, auto enhance

ALBUM
- Favourites, your own albums, import and share

Your photos stay on your device. Optional small AI models download once.

## Review notes (Apple / Google reviewers)
Hazen is a camera app. It needs camera and microphone access to take photos and record video, and the photo library to import media. No account or login is needed. AI models are downloaded once from Google storage and jsDelivr, then run on the device.
