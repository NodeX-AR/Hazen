06 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Hazen really free?

Yes. You can use it for personal or commercial work at no cost. The license lets you use and share unmodified copies, but not modify, repackage or resell it.

Where do my photos go?

Nowhere. Photos and videos stay on your device. There is no server, no account and no analytics. The AI models download once from Google storage and jsDelivr, and no photos are sent.

Does it work offline?

Yes, after the first run. Only the first download of each AI model needs a connection.

Is it open source?

No. The code is public so you can read and audit it, but it is under a use-only license. See the LICENSE file.

How do I save photos on iPhone?

Photos stay in the Hazen album. Tap Share, then Save Image, to put them in Photos.

Something broke. Who do I tell?

Open an issue on GitHub, or email aswanthr82@gmail.com. For security problems please email privately.
