FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Is Hazen free?

Yes. Use it for personal or commercial work at no cost. The license lets you use and share unmodified copies, but not modify, repackage or resell it.

Where do my photos go?

Nowhere. They stay on your device. There is no server and no account. The AI models ship inside the app, or are saved to your device once during setup, and no photos are ever sent.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The apps carry the AI inside them. In the browser, a one-time setup saves it to your device, and after that nothing is downloaded again.

Will it run on an older phone?

Yes, that is what it is tuned for. Hazen detects budget devices and switches to Low-end mode: 720p, 24 frames per second and lighter graphics. You can switch it in Settings, Pro.

Is it open source?

No. The code is public so you can read and audit it, under a use-only license.

How do I save photos on iPhone?

Photos stay in the Hazen album. Tap Share, then Save Image, to put them in Photos.

Something broke. Who do I tell?

Open an issue on GitHub or email aswanthr82@gmail.com. For security problems please email privately.
