# Features

## Camera
Photo, Video, Portrait, Ultra (multi-frame 4K) and Night. Lens-ring zoom, exposure, focus, white balance, histogram, zebra, focus peaking, level, grids. Looks and custom `.cube` LUTs. **Auto Enhance** is a separate layer, so it never overwrites your chosen look. AI helpers: face lock, smile and gesture shutter, blink guard, portrait blur. Optional **watermark** (Settings > Hazen), small, bottom right, on by default.

## Editor
- Photos: crop, straighten, adjust, looks, sharpen/soften, draw, text, stickers, AI background blur, **cut out subject (PNG)**, smart frame, non-destructive Auto Enhance.
- Videos: trim, speed, colour, fades, text, and a **Fairlight-style mixer**: fader, pan, 3-band EQ, compressor, live level meter.

## Album
Favourites, custom albums, import, multi-select share.

## Privacy
No account, no analytics, no uploads. AI models are downloaded once from Google storage and jsDelivr.
